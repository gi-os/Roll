## Roll v2.53 — six words instead of a sentence

**"Click wheel to unlock."** The notice a locked dial puts on the panel was naming the settings
route as well as the click, which is a sentence to read at the moment you are looking at a dial
that just refused to move. You want to know what to press. Where the setting lives is a thing to go
and find later, and it is still in Settings › Keys.

Nothing else changes.
